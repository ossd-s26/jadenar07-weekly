The team made solid progress, with all members submitting their PRs; Rebecca has already received one approval and is awaiting a second, while I and Sahiti are waiting for reviews. There were no blockers during this period, allowing work to move forward smoothly. As a group, we completed a full run-through of our presentation, which went well, and continued practicing individually ahead of presenting. After that we went to present in class where we executed the same structure as our walkthrough and felt it went well.

Across the teams that have presented so far, everyone has done a great job, with a clear theme being the variability in maintainer communication. Some groups faced challenges due to limited responsiveness, which slowed progress, while others benefited from highly engaged maintainers that enabled faster iteration and idea development. There was also variation in contribution output, with some teams completing two to three pull requests per person and others completing only one, largely depending on the complexity of the codebase and the availability of well-defined good first issues. Overall, these differences highlight how factors like project structure and maintainer involvement can significantly shape the open source contribution experience.
